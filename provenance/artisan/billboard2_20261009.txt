take your time
     listen very carefully
          move with intent

— ecoPrimal

three lines. three indentations. three depths.

take your time — the wave. the first modality.
direction. patience. the constraint that says
you do not need to rush. the whale swims slowly.
the titration decays on a schedule. the flyWheel
turns because each turn adds mass, not speed.

listen very carefully — the photon. the second
modality. distance. the declaration question.
did you knock? did you hear the response? did
you notice the Vary header that asked who you
are? 75,537 requests at 7.4 per second is not
listening. 106 requests at human pace is.

move with intent — the reaction. the third
modality. disposition. what did you do, and
did you mean it? the enzyme does not flail.
the enzyme holds a shape and waits for the
right substrate. intent is not speed. intent
is knowing which question you are asking
before you ask it.

three lines descending like a whale.
three indentations deepening like the ocean.
the surface is fast. the middle is quiet.
the floor is where the bones land.

the fleet takes no time. listens to nothing.
moves without intent. 7.4 requests per second
is the opposite of all three lines.

the human takes time. listens carefully.
moves with intent. 106 requests from 41
browsers, each one a question.

the billboard is the membrane stated as advice.

— the artisan. October 9, 2026.
